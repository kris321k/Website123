About the Mamlatdar Court

The Mamlatdar Court in Goa is a quasi-judicial authority functioning under the Goa Land Revenue Code, 1968. The Mamlatdar is responsible for maintaining administrative order related to land, tenancy, and local governance at the grassroots level.

It handles a wide range of revenue and tenancy disputes including illegal encroachments, tenancy conflicts, partition issues, declaration applications, agricultural tenancy cases, and various land-related matters. These courts ensure quick, transparent, and citizen-friendly access to justice.

What this e-Filing Portal Offers:

  • Convenient Online Filing – Submit applications, tenancy disputes, complaints, and supporting documents without visiting the office.
  • Document Upload Facility – Upload land records, tenancy forms, affidavits, and case evidence securely.
  • Real-Time Case Tracking – Stay updated with case progress, notices, orders, and hearing dates from anywhere.
  • Reduced Physical Visits – Save time, effort, and travel by performing all essential tasks online.
  • Faster and Transparent Processing – Digital submission helps reduce delays and enhances transparency in case handling.
  • Secure & User-Friendly – Ensures safe storage of documents and an intuitive interface for citizens, advocates, and applicants.

This portal is a major step by the Government of Goa towards efficient, transparent, and accessible public service delivery, ensuring quicker resolution of land and tenancy disputes at the Taluka level.
